Our Story

Baking Little Moments of Sweet Joy

Cozy Bakes by Wardah is a little home bakery in Mauritius, created with love, care, and purpose. We craft comforting treats using thoughtfully chosen ingredients, mindful portions, and more sustainable practices wherever possible. From eco-conscious packaging to reducing waste, we believe small choices can make a difference. Most importantly, we care about your well-being and happiness, creating delicious bakes that are made with quality, care, and a little everyday magic.

We believe that every celebration—whether a quiet afternoon tea or a grand milestone event—deserves a centerpiece made with genuine love and thoughtful care.

Help & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

For standard cupcakes, cookies, and donuts, please place orders 24–48 hours in advance. For custom celebration cakes, we recommend booking 5–7 days prior.

Are gluten-free options available?

Yes! We offer gluten-free flour options upon request when placing your order.

Where do you deliver in Mauritius?

We offer pickup options as well as direct courier delivery across major districts in Mauritius. Delivery fees vary by region.
